# Patience Under the Stars: The Story of Tilly

Once upon a time, Tilly the young turtle lived near a sparkling pond. The sun was sinking low, the sky turned soft pink, and everything felt calm and cosy. Tilly the young turtle loved evenings like this, when the whole world slowed down.

On this special evening, Tilly the young turtle went for a little walk with Odo the duck. Along the way, Tilly the young turtle planted a tiny seed and wanted it to grow right away. Odo the duck whispered, "Good things take time." So they waited, watered the seed, and one morning a bright flower popped up. It was a small moment, but it felt very big and very warm.

As the first stars began to twinkle, Tilly the young turtle and Odo the duck waved goodnight. Tilly the young turtle walked home slowly, feeling happy and proud. A soft blanket was waiting, and a gentle breeze hummed a lullaby. Tilly the young turtle yawned a big, sleepy yawn, snuggled in tight, closed both eyes, and drifted off to a peaceful sleep.

The end. Sweet dreams.

Moral: Good things come to those who wait.
